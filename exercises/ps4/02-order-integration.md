## Statement

Find the order of integration of each process. Innovations $\varepsilon_t$ and $\eta_t$ are independent white noises.

1. $x_t=\rho x_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t$, $|\rho|<1$.
2. $x_t=x_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t+\theta_1\varepsilon_{t-1}+\cdots+\theta_q\varepsilon_{t-q}$, assuming the MA polynomial does not cancel the unit root.
3. $x_t=\mu_t+x_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t$, with $\mu_t=\mu_{t-1}+\eta_t$.
4. $x_t=1.5x_{t-1}-0.5x_{t-2}+\varepsilon_t$.
