## Statement

Let $\varepsilon_t\overset{i.i.d.}{\sim}\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2)$ and consider
$$
(1-0.9L)(1+0.5L)y_t=\varepsilon_t,
$$
$$
(1-L)x_t=-0.9x_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t,
$$
$$
(1-0.5L)w_t=0.5w_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t.
$$

State whether each assertion is true or false and justify your answer.

1. $y_t$ is integrated of order one.
2. $x_t$ is covariance stationary.
3. $w_t$ is covariance stationary.
4. $\Delta y_t$ is covariance stationary.
5. $\Delta w_t$ is covariance stationary.
