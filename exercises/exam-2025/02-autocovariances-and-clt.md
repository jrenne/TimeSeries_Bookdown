## Statement

A covariance-stationary process $y_t$ has autocovariances
$$
\gamma_0=3,\quad \gamma_1=1.5,\quad \gamma_2=1,\quad \gamma_3=0.5,
\quad\text{and}\quad \gamma_h=0\ \text{for }h\ge4.
$$
Let $\bar y_T=T^{-1}\sum_{t=1}^T y_t$, where $T$ is large.

State whether each assertion is true or false and justify your answer.

1. The process can be a nondegenerate AR(4).
2. $\mathbb{V}ar(\bar y_T)=3$.
3. The correlation between $y_{t-3}$ and $y_{t-5}$ is zero.
4. If $\bar y_T=1$ and $T=900$, an approximate 95% confidence interval for $\mathbb E(y_t)$ does not include zero.
5. $\mathbb E(y_{t+1})=\mathbb E(y_{t-1})$.
