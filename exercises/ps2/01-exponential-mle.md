## Statement

Customer inter-arrival times $y_1,\ldots,y_T$ are i.i.d. exponential with **scale** parameter $\lambda>0$:
$$f(y;\lambda)=\lambda^{-1}\exp(-y/\lambda),\qquad y\geq0.$$

1. Write the log-likelihood.
2. Derive the maximum-likelihood estimator of $\lambda$.
3. Compute the Fisher information and the estimator's asymptotic standard error.
