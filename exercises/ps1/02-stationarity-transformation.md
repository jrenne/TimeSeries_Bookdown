## Statement

Let $y_t=y_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t$, where $\{\varepsilon_t\}$ is white noise, and define $x_t=y_t-y_{t-1}$.

1. Is $y_t$ covariance stationary?
2. Express $x_t$ in terms of the innovation and determine whether $x_t$ is covariance stationary.
3. State the order of integration of $y_t$.
