## Statement

For each stationary process in Exercise \@ref(exr:ex-stationarity):

1. Derive the first four Wold coefficients $\psi_0,\ldots,\psi_3$ in $y_t-\mu=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\psi_j\varepsilon_{t-j}$.
2. Compute the three-step-ahead conditional forecast $\mathbb E_t(y_{t+3})$.
3. Compute the corresponding forecast-error variance.

Take the innovations to have variance $\sigma^2$ and state clearly which information set is used.
