## Statement

Let $\varepsilon_t\overset{i.i.d.}{\sim}\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2)$ and
$$
(1-0.5L^2)x_t=0.1+0.5x_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t,
$$
$$
(1+1.1L)(1-0.5L)(1+0.4L)y_t=\varepsilon_t,
$$
$$
z_t+0.8z_{t-1}=(1+L+L^2)\varepsilon_t.
$$

State whether each assertion is true or false and justify your answer.

1. $x_t$ is covariance stationary.
2. $y_t$ is covariance stationary.
3. $z_t$ is covariance stationary.
4. $\gamma_1(z)=-0.8\gamma_0(z)$.
5. $\operatorname{Var}(z_t+0.8z_{t-1})=3\sigma^2$.
