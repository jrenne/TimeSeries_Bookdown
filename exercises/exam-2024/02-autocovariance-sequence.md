## Statement

A covariance-stationary process has $\gamma_0=5$, $\gamma_1=4$, $\gamma_2=3$, $\gamma_3=2$, $\gamma_4=1$, and $\gamma_h=0$ for $h>4$.

State whether each assertion is true or false and justify your answer.

1. The process can be a nondegenerate AR(4).
2. It could be $y_t=\varepsilon_t+\varepsilon_{t-1}+\varepsilon_{t-2}+\varepsilon_{t-3}+\varepsilon_{t-4}$ with $\operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon_t)=1$.
3. $\operatorname{Var}(y_t)=\sum_{h=0}^4\gamma_h$.
4. $\operatorname{Corr}(y_{t-1},y_{t-4})=2/5$.
5. Necessarily, $\mathbb E(y_t)=\gamma_0$.
